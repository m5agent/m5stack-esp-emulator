Why the fix closes the specific implementation gap:
- The real GPIO OUT_SEL register is committed before the Unit notification;
  LEDC refresh remains present. The hook matches layout, yellow pin and an
  existing FlashLight instance, and therefore does not revoke another pin,
  another layout or non-FlashLight state.
- Route mutations in both directions discard old low timestamps, qualification,
  pulses, all three deadlines/timers and increment epoch. Restoring GPIO256
  cannot expose prior Torch/Flash active state or a constructor reset timer.
- Unsupported/mismatched actual pad callbacks still record the actual edge but
  immediately perform complete observation loss and return; they cannot create
  a new low interval while observation is unsupported. Timer-side loss uses the
  same complete invalidation.
- A fresh supported low interval can restore known-off and a new actual program
  can restore active. No route mutation increments edge/rising/decode counts.
  last-program and edge history remain diagnostic history, not active output.
