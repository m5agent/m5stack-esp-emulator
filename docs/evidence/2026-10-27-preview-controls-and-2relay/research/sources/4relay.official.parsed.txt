Unit 4Relay
SKU:U097
Description
Unit 4Relay is a high-voltage drive unit that integrates 4-channel relay control. It is based on the STM32F030F4P6 microcontroller and uses the I2C bus to independently control four mechanical relays, supporting high-voltage switching of AC 250V / DC 28V. The unit communicates via a Grove HY2.0-4P interface and features physical isolation, programmable status indicator LEDs, and overload capability. It also adopts a LEGO-compatible mounting hole design, making it easy to integrate with LEGO structures or secure using screws. This unit is suitable for high-voltage load switching scenarios such as smart home applications, industrial control, and power management of high-power equipment.
Features
Channel Count: 4-channel independent relay control
Load Capacity: AC 250V@10A or DC 28V@10A
Status Indication: Independent programmable LED indicator for each channel
Isolation Feature: Physical isolation between high-voltage and low-voltage sides
2 x LEGO-compatible mounting holes
