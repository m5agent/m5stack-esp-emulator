## Scope and limitations

- In both real runs, burst-B and burst-C were superseded while pending, before UART synchronization. They have no actual UART execution/history. These sessions validate latest-pending-wins and active A/D cancellation; they do not validate cancellation midway through a resource transfer.
- The recorder preserves raw response bytes and ordered response headers, but not request headers. Request-key use is bound to the frozen runner source; this is not an independent wire capture of Idempotency-Key.
- This review establishes the explicit CoreS3/PIR and CoreS3/Relay HTTP, guest, UART, framebuffer and QOM paths for the exact recorded firmware/runtime. It does not add live-browser, other-board, other-Unit or physical-hardware acceptance.
- Cleanup mode is interrupt-only. Application-level peripheral effects may require an explicit subsequent action; a fresh REPL prompt is not a transactional rollback.
